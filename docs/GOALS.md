# Goals

1. Prove a trustworthy paid education marketplace for Morocco with the smallest complete discovery-to-settlement journey.
2. Let verified teachers publish classes without needing an existing audience.
3. Let students/families understand price, schedule, teacher, access, cancellation, and learning delivery before paying.
4. Keep payment, entitlement, private-file access, refunds, and teacher settlement correct and auditable.
5. Keep infrastructure proportional to the pilot and avoid vendor lock-in where it would make future changes expensive.
6. Enable Ali and Anass to develop safely in parallel with independent coding agents.

Accepted product rules: [MVP scope](product/mvp-scope.md) and [accepted ADRs](adr/README.md). Open founder decisions: [founder decision log](product/founder-decision-log.md).
